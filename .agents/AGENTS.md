# Project Rules for Wasmee

## Translation & Editing Guidelines

- **Translation Editing & Simplification**: Any article, documentation, or public communication that is translated into multiple languages must undergo a thorough editorial review.
- **Goal of Review**:
  - **Phrase Correctness**: Ensure all technical terms, idioms, and sentences are natural, grammatically correct, and contextually appropriate in the target language.
  - **Simplification**: Simplify complex technical phrasing to make it highly readable and engaging for the target audience.
  - **Review Step**: Do not publish auto-translated content directly. Always review and polish the translated text for clarity, tone, and readability.
